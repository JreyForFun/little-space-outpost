# Art Direction: "Cozy cosmic toy box"

The world should feel like a glowing toy diorama: warm, colourful and alive. It is never grey or
flat. Every asset is generated by script (`tools/blender/render_assets.py`,
`tools/compose_sheets.py`) or drawn in code (`src/ui*.js`).

## Shapes and lighting

- **Cel shading:** 3 flat tones (shadow, base, light) with a 3–4 px ink outline (inverted hull).
- **Lights:** a warm key light from the upper left and a cool ambient fill. Emissive parts
  (windows, lamps, screens) glow, and the game adds soft additive halos.
- **Proportions:** chubby and toy-like. Bevelled, rounded forms, with no sharp industrial detail.

## Characters

- **Crew:** chibi proportions with a head as big as the body. The helmet's front is open, so the
  face is always visible: big eyes with highlights, rosy cheeks and a readable mouth. Each
  astronaut has a distinct skin tone, hair and suit accent:
  - Rio (engineer): coral;
  - Sage (botanist): green;
  - Lumi (geologist): violet.
- **Crew expressions:** happy, worried and tired. Portraits react to the mission state.
- **ASTRO:** a round white robot coach with a navy screen face and glowing cyan eyes. Expressions:
  happy, talk, wow and worried. ASTRO always hovers, with a gentle bob and a jet glow.

## UI

- **Glass panels:** deep navy at 85% opacity, a 2 px light-cyan border and rounded 18 px corners.
- **Dialogue bubbles:** white with ink outlines.
- **Buttons:** chunky and rounded, with a top highlight and a drop shadow. They squash when
  pressed and play a click sound.
- **Fonts:** Fredoka for headings and buttons, Nunito for body text (both bundled under OFL).
- **Motion:** every value change animates (count up, pulse). Warnings pulse red with a soft alarm.
