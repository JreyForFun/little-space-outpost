REAL SCIENCE, CLEARLY LABELED

Space weather can change a mission plan.

The game introduces solar activity and radiation as real planning concerns, and points players to NASA’s DONKI space-weather database.

Honest data note: The solar alert in this prototype is randomly generated for practice. It is not a live forecast and does not fetch current DONKI data.
